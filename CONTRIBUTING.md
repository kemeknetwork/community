# Participating

This repository is a conversation, not a codebase. "Contributing" mostly means
posting well.

## Before you post

**Search first.** Someone may have asked already, and an existing thread with
an answer is faster than a new one without.

**Pick the right category.** Wrong-category posts get moved, which costs a day.

| Category | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| [Q&A](https://github.com/kemeknetwork/community/discussions/categories/q-a) | Anything with a right answer — how do I, why does it, is this broken |
| [Ideas](https://github.com/kemeknetwork/community/discussions/categories/ideas) | Something that doesn't exist yet and you wish did |
| [Show and tell](https://github.com/kemeknetwork/community/discussions/categories/show-and-tell) | What you built, shipped, or figured out |
| [General](https://github.com/kemeknetwork/community/discussions/categories/general) | Everything that fits nowhere else |
| [Announcements](https://github.com/kemeknetwork/community/discussions/categories/announcements) | Read-only — maintainers post, everyone comments |

**Security issues go to [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md), never here.** Account and
billing matters go to [SUPPORT.md](./SUPPORT.md).

## Writing a post that gets answered

A title that describes the problem beats one that describes your mood. "Export
fails silently on files over 10 MB" gets read; "help!!" does not.

In the body, cover four things:

1. **What you were trying to do** — the goal, not just the step you got stuck on.
2. **What you did** — exact steps, in order, that someone else could repeat.
3. **What happened** — the actual error, message, or wrong output. Paste it as
   text in a code fence rather than a screenshot, so it can be searched and
   quoted. Screenshots are great *in addition*, especially for visual bugs.
4. **What you expected instead.**

Then add the context that narrows it down: product and version, browser and OS,
whether it ever worked before, and what you already tried.

**One topic per thread.** Two questions in one post reliably gets one answered.

**Redact before you paste.** Strip API keys, tokens, passwords, session
cookies, customer names, and email addresses from logs and screenshots. Assume
anything posted here is permanent and indexed — because it is.

## After you post

- **Mark the answer.** In Q&A, marking the comment that solved it is what makes
  the thread useful to the next person.
- **Say what worked**, even when you solved it yourself. "Never mind, fixed it"
  with no explanation is the most frustrating thread on the internet.
- **Bump a quiet thread** rather than reposting. Silence is usually a small team
  being busy, not a decision.

## Answering other people

Answers from anyone are welcome and are how this space earns its keep. Say when
you are guessing. Correct people kindly, including us. If a thread turns out to
be a real bug, saying so plainly is more useful than a workaround that hides it.

## Changes to this repository

Spotted a dead link, a typo, or routing that sent you the wrong way?
[Open an issue](https://github.com/kemeknetwork/community/issues) — that
tracker is for defects in *these documents*, and that is the only thing it is
for. A problem with a KeMeK product is a discussion, not an issue.

Pull requests are welcome against the files here too — typos, broken links,
unclear wording, or a genuinely better explanation in any of these documents.

- Branch from `dev`, open the PR against `dev`.
- Keep the scope to one thing.
- Documentation only. This repository holds no product source, so there is no
  build and nothing to run.

Everything you post or submit is covered by the
[Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
